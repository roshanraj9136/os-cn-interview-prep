# 12-15 LPA companies (2025-26 data)

Is page par real placement lists aur interview experiences se data hai: kaunsi companies freshers ko lagbhag 12-15 LPA de rahi hain, aur unke interview mein OS/CN kaise aaya. Har number ke saath source diya hai (neeche "Sources" dekho).

::: warning Ye numbers kaise padhein
- Ye ek-do colleges ki lists hain. Same company doosre college mein ya doosre role ke liye alag CTC de sakti hai.
- **CTC** (cost to company) mein base salary, bonus, benefits sab jude hote hain. Monthly in-hand isse kam hota hai (PF, tax, variable pay katke). Joining bonus sirf ek baar milta hai.
- Kai campus offers "Intern" role ke naam se aate hain (internship ke baad full-time). Table mein role waise hi likha hai jaise college list mein hai.
- Data last checked: 30 October 2026. Apne college ke T&P notice se hamesha confirm karo.
:::

## Company type ke hisaab se focus

| Company type | Examples | OS/CN mein kis par zyada focus karein |
|---|---|---|
| Banks ki tech teams | Wells Fargo, Barclays, IDFC First Bank, Deutsche Bank | Process vs thread, zombie/orphan, deadlock, TCP vs UDP, routing basics, google.com flow. Saath mein SQL/DBMS. |
| Networking / telecom software | Nokia, NetCracker, NetGear | CN deep: OSI, routing (R1 se R3), DHCP, subnetting, TCP handshake aur sockets, packet flow. |
| Product / software companies | BMC, Ansys, PubMatic, Brillio, Epsilon, Flexera | OS fundamentals (process, memory, synchronization) + CN basics (HTTP, DNS, TCP/UDP). |
| Premium tracks | Infosys SP, TCS Prime | Coding round sabse important; interview mein OOPs, DBMS, OS ke basics. |

Networking companies ke liye "CN deep" wali line ek general salah hai (unka kaam hi networking hai); uske liye Nokia wala experience upar diya hai.
