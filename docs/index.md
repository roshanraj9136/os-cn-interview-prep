## Kahan se shuru karein?

1. [Yahan se shuru karo](/start-here) padho (5 minute). Isme bataya hai ki interview mein OS/CN kaise puchha jaata hai aur answer kaise dena hai.
2. [7 din ka study plan](/study-plan) kholo aur roz ka checklist follow karo.
3. Har page ke end mein Quick Quiz do. 80% se upar aaye to agla page.
